1. Compatable is guidance, not medical advice

Compatable is an informational tool. It reads menus and ingredient text and estimates whether dishes may conflict with the dietary restrictions you've selected. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and must never be your only line of defense against a food allergy.

Menus change. Kitchens substitute ingredients, share fryers, and cross-contaminate. Text recognition and AI analysis can miss things or be wrong. A dish marked safe can still contain your allergen.

Always confirm directly with restaurant staff or the product packaging before eating, especially for severe allergies. If you carry emergency medication such as epinephrine, continue to rely on it and your doctor's plan, not this app.
